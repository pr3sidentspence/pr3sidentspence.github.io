pastforward_2026_osm.geojson
============================

This database is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

It contains:

  - Building footprints © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the ODbL.
    https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
    (the source way/relation of each footprint is in "osm_id")

  - Information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Winnipeg:
      * Assessment Parcels (data.winnipeg.ca dataset d4mq-wa44)
      * Historical Resources (data.winnipeg.ca dataset ptpx-kgiu)
    https://data.winnipeg.ca/stories/s/rwzh-4ijx

It is an independent database, published alongside (not merged into)
pastforward_2026_assessment.geojson and the other PastForward data files, which
contain no OpenStreetMap data and are not covered by this licence.

Built by matching/merge_assessment.py.
